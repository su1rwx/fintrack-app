FinTrack Privacy Policy

FinTrack is a personal app that stores your finance data only on your own devices and in your own Google Drive.

What the app accesses

If you connect Google Drive, FinTrack requests the drive.file permission. This lets the app see and manage only the folder and file that it creates itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

How your data is protected

Your data is encrypted with AES-256 using a master password that only you know, before it is saved on your device or uploaded to Google Drive. The app has no servers. We do not receive, collect, store or sell your data.

Sharing and analytics

Your data is not shared with anyone. The app uses no advertising or analytics services.

Deleting your data

You can delete the app and the FinTrack folder in your Google Drive at any time. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
